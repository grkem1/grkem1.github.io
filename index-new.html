Who I Am

I work in Linux on System z Distribution Test at IBM, focusing on Linux behavior on IBM Z platforms. I bring a deep technical curiosity and first-principles approach to understanding complex system interactions.

Self-driven, quick starter, passionate engineer with a curious mind who enjoys solving complex and challenging real-world problems. I strive to understand systems at a fundamental level, from OS internals to hardware architecture.

What I Do

I work on distribution testing and validation for enterprise Linux systems on IBM Z, investigating OS internals, virtualization, processor resource management, and performance analysis across RHEL, SLES, and Ubuntu distributions.

My work involves deep dives into LPAR architecture, CPU polarization, hypervisor scheduling, virtualization stacks, and kernel interfaces. I'm passionate about automation and exploring AI-assisted engineering workflows.
